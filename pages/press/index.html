Descriptions

In one sentence

uSkyBlock is a free, open-source Skyblock plugin for Paper and Spigot servers, first released in 2012 as Ultimate SkyBlock.

Short, about 50 words

uSkyBlock is a free, open-source Skyblock plugin for Minecraft servers on Paper and Spigot, developed since 2012. Players start on a small island in the void and grow it, alone or with friends, through 56 challenges in 6 ranks. Parties, island levels, warps, biomes and a skyblock Nether are built in.

Long, about 150 words

uSkyBlock brings the classic Skyblock game to Minecraft servers on Paper and Spigot. Each player or party starts on a small island in the void with a tree and a chest, and builds it up into farms, houses and whole cities in the sky.

Challenges carry the game: 56 of them in 6 ranks, from the first cobblestone generator onwards, with island levels and a top list to compare islands. Parties, warps, visitor controls, island biomes and a skyblock Nether are built in, so a server runs Skyblock with one plugin rather than a set of addons. Admins can change the challenges, island templates, limits and every message, and the plugin is available in 54 languages.

uSkyBlock is free and open source under the GNU GPLv3, with no paid version. Talabrek first released it as Ultimate SkyBlock in 2012. A line of maintainers has carried it on since, and it has more than 500,000 downloads.

At a glance

  • Classic Skyblock: one small island in the void, grown alone or with a party
  • 56 challenges in 6 ranks, island levels and an island top list
  • Parties, warps, visitor trust, ban and lock, island biomes and a skyblock Nether, all in one plugin
  • Challenges, island templates, limits and messages can all be changed; PlaceholderAPI, Vault and a developer API
  • Available in 54 languages
  • Free and open source (GNU GPLv3), with no paid version, and maintained for current Minecraft

Good to know

  • The name. uSkyBlock is short for Ultimate SkyBlock. On SpigotMC it is listed as "uSkyBlock Revived".
  • Where it runs. Spigot, Paper and Paper forks such as Purpur.
  • It is maintained. The current release runs on Minecraft 26.3, and new releases follow new Minecraft versions.
